# Store listing draft

**Title:** Racing: Learn & Fun

**Short description:** Pick a car, follow spoken clues, and learn letters, numbers, colors and more!

**Full description:**

Ready, set, learn and race! Choose your colorful car and take a gentle drive through five learning worlds. Listen to the clue, steer toward the matching object, and celebrate each new discovery.

• 10 illustrated cars, from Sunny Bug to Grape Rocket and Cherry Firetruck.
• Alphabet Avenue: learn A–Z.
• Rainbow Road: discover 10 colors.
• Number Highway: practice numbers 1–100.
• Safari Speedway: meet 20 animals.
• Fruit Garden: recognize 12 fruits.
• 105 levels with repeated learning, accuracy-based stars, scores, and combos.
• Spoken English clues, encouraging sounds, and cheerful celebrations.
• Gentle driving option for extra thinking time.
• Offline play from the very first launch, including voice prompts.
• No ads, purchases, accounts, or tracking.

Designed for curious young learners with grown-up guidance. Tap a lane, swipe, or use the large steering buttons. Progress stays on your device, and favorite levels can be replayed any time.

**Release notes:** First release: five learning worlds, ten cars, 105 levels, offline voice clues, local progress, accuracy stars, and phone/tablet layouts.
