# Racing: Learn & Fun — privacy policy draft

**Publisher: complete the legal developer name and support email before publishing this policy.**

Effective date: 20 November 2026. Applies to version 2.0.0.

Racing: Learn & Fun is a children's educational racing game for preschoolers, with separate 7+ and 13+ modes. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no advertisements, analytics, accounts, purchases, social features, or third-party tracking SDKs. The Android app requests no permissions and has no internet access.

The app saves the selected car, sound/voice/speed settings, best scores, and earned stars locally on the device. These are not sent to the developer. Android cloud backup is disabled. A parent can reset progress from Settings → Parent corner, or erase all saved settings and progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. The offline browser edition uses the browser's local storage; clearing browser data removes it.

All game pictures, sounds, and spoken learning prompts are packaged with the game. No microphone access or speech recording is used. The app does not ask children to provide names, email addresses, photos, location, or other personal information.

If a parent independently contacts the publisher for support outside the game, the publisher may receive information included in that message. The publisher must describe its support-data handling and provide a valid contact email here before publication.

Changes to this policy will be reflected in a revised effective date. The publisher is responsible for maintaining a public policy URL and accurate Play Console disclosures.
